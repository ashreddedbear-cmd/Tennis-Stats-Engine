For each bucket, the chart should compare:

* Predicted Win % (average prediction from your model)
* Actual Win % (what actually happened historically)

If your model predicts 71–75% and those players actually win 73% of the time, that’s an excellent bucket. If it predicts 81–85% but those players only win 74% of the time, then that bucket is overconfident.
