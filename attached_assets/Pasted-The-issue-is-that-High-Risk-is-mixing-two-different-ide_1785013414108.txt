Upset Risk should answer:

* Moderate Confidence with Low Upset Risk
* Low Confidence with Extreme Upset Risk

Do not automatically force the recommendation label to match the Upset Risk label.

Intended Meaning of Each Category

Highest Confidence

Use only when there is strong, reliable and consistent evidence across several independent signals. Data Quality must be sufficient, model disagreement must be limited, and no major unresolved risk flags may exist.

High Confidence

Use when the predicted winner has a meaningful, well-supported advantage, but the match does not satisfy every Highest Confidence requirement.

Moderate Confidence

Use when the model identifies a real advantage, but evidence is mixed, incomplete or the match remains reasonably competitive.

Low Confidence

Use when the model projects a winner but the advantage is weak, fragile or heavily affected by uncertainty.

Insufficient Edge

Use when the evidence does not reliably separate the players. This should act as a pass or abstain recommendation rather than a forced confident pick.
