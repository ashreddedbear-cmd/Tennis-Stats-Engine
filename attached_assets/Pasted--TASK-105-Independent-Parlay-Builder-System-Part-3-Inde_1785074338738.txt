# TASK 105 – Independent Parlay Builder System (Part 3)

# Independent Builder Scoring Engine

The Parlay Builder must calculate every score independently.

It must never reference, normalize, scale, transform, or inherit any Prediction Engine score.

Builder scores must come entirely from Builder evidence.

---

# Independent Builder Factors

Each leg must receive a score from every validation factor.

Minimum required factors:

1. Tennis Abstract Overall Elo
2. Tennis Abstract Surface Elo
3. UTR Rating Advantage
4. Recent Form
5. Surface Performance
6. Serve Advantage
7. Return Advantage
8. Hold Percentage
9. Break Percentage
10. Opponent Quality
11. Strength of Schedule
12. Head-to-Head
13. Ranking Trend
14. Tournament Experience
15. Surface Experience
16. Travel Fatigue
17. Days Rest
18. Injury Risk
19. Retirement History
20. Market Consensus
21. Historical Consistency
22. Historical Volatility
23. Data Coverage
24. Data Freshness
25. Source Agreement

Each factor produces its own score.

No factor should directly determine KEEP or REMOVE.

# Validation Score

Calculate:

Validation Score

Range:

0–100

Represents:

"How much independent evidence supports this prediction?"

Higher = Better

Never use Prediction Engine probability.

Never convert Engine Safety Score.

---

# Risk Score

Calculate:

Risk Score

Range:

0–100

Higher = More Risk

Increase Risk when:

• Sources disagree

• Surface mismatch

• Weak recent form

• High volatility

• Retirement history

• Injury concern

• Market disagreement

• Fatigue

• Travel

• Missing data

Lower Risk when:

• Sources agree

• Good surface history

• Strong Elo advantage

• Strong UTR advantage

• Strong recent form

• Good consistency

---

# Reliability Grade

B

C

D

F

Reliability depends on BOTH:

Evidence

AND

Data Quality

Example:

Validation = 92

Coverage = 35%

Reliability should NOT be A.

Low data coverage must reduce reliability.

# Parlay Grade

Builder assigns:

Elite

Strong

Moderate

Weak

Reject

Grades are determined ONLY by Builder calculations.

---

# Removal Probability

Builder calculates:

Removal Probability

0–100%

This means:

Probability that the leg should be removed from the parlay.

NOT

Probability the player loses.

This distinction must be clearly shown.

---

# KEEP / BORDERLINE / REMOVE

Builder decides independently.

KEEP

Strong evidence.

Low risk.

Good agreement.

Good coverage.

BORDERLINE

Mixed evidence.

Average risk.

Some disagreement.

Missing information.

REMOVE

Poor evidence.

High risk.

Multiple strong sources disagree.

Critical validation problems.

---

# Builder Decision Logic

Builder combines:

Risk Score

Critical Flags

Decision:

KEEP

BORDERLINE

Decision must never use Prediction Engine recommendation.

---

# Critical Flags

Immediately reduce Builder confidence when:

Player mismatch

Tournament mismatch

Surface mismatch

Missing opponent

Duplicate player

Incomplete draw

Unknown tournament

Unknown surface

Recent retirement

Confirmed injury

Large market disagreement

Extremely stale data

Every critical flag must appear on the leg card.

---

# Decision Reasons

Every Builder decision must include between 3 and 6 evidence-based explanations.

KEEP Example

• Tennis Abstract Elo strongly favors this player.

• UTR agrees with the prediction.

• Excellent recent hard-court form.

• Market consensus supports this selection.

REMOVE Example

• Surface Elo favors opponent.

• UTR projects opponent advantage.

• Market moved sharply against this player.

• Recent form is significantly weaker.

Generic explanations are prohibited.

Every reason must correspond to an actual scoring factor.
