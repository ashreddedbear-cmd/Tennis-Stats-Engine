**Task — Accelerate Market-Odds Validation via Historical Data, and Confirm the Live Pipeline Actually Recovers**

**What & Why**

Two things, both time-sensitive for different reasons:

1. **Market-odds validation is blocked on live data that isn't accumulating.** Paper-trade rows with real odds are stuck at 184 of the 500 needed for Task #85. Diagnosis just confirmed why: the walk-forward job's `getPlayerMatches` calls tripped the API-Tennis circuit breaker open, which broke `getUpcomingFixtures` — so the entire live paper-trading loop has been dark (zero new fixtures locked, zero new predictions of any kind, not just market-odds ones). Real historical odds data already exists in `historical_matches` via `tennisDataCoUkBackfill.ts` (`raw_source->'_marketOdds'`, ~11,007 rows spanning 2016–2020) and is completely unaffected by this outage — it doesn't touch fixtures, the circuit breaker, or live provider calls at all.

2. **The circuit-breaker recovery is currently assumed, not confirmed.** The working theory is "it resets on its next health check once the walk-forward finishes" — but that's an inference, not something read directly from the reset/half-open logic. Given the walk-forward and live paper-trading now have a confirmed interference pattern (one job's API load breaks the other job's fixture fetching), this needs to be verified explicitly, not assumed, or it could silently still be dark hours from now — and this exact conflict will recur every time a heavy backfill runs concurrently with live trading unless it's addressed structurally.

- Run it, report accuracy delta and log-loss delta at whatever sample size this reaches (should comfortably exceed 500 given ~11,007 available rows).
- Cross-check against the live paper-trade arm once it resumes accumulating (Part B) — flag if the two disagree meaningfully.
