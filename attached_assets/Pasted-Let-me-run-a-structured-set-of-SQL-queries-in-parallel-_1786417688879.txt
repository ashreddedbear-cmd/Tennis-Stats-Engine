The overfit question answered directly

Yes, the pipeline's validation corpus was effectively overfit to 2022 conditions, and it matters.

The 2022 corpus was:

90% ATP/WTA main draw (tour-level), biased toward Grand Slams and 250/500 events
0% ITF, 10% Challenger
Average validation score of 58.0, with 28% of rows at grade A or B
The Elite (adj≥58) and KEEP (val≥62) thresholds were set and evaluated against that sample. The 70.1% accuracy on KEEP-grade rows in training was real — but it was measuring the model on the most predictable subset of tennis that exists.

What has changed: The evaluation pool in 2024-2025 is majority Challenger/ITF, where inherent predictability is lower, player histories are thinner, and the model receives lower validation scores even for the same nominal tier. The Grand Slam sample (which most visibly anchored the 70% figure) has itself degraded ~6-7pp as input quality at those events declined.

What this does NOT mean: The KEEP gate is not broken. The ~10pp separation between val≥62 and val<62 is consistent in both train (70.1% vs 59.1%) and test (65.4% vs 55.1%) halves. The structure of the gate works. But the absolute level it delivers is overstated by approximately 5pp for conditions since 2024. Users and any threshold documentation should expect ~65% (not 70%) for KEEP-grade picks under current corpus conditions.

The Elite threshold (adj≥58) similarly still provides real lift, but the absolute implied accuracy was calibrated against a 2022 baseline that no longer represents the live pool.

Not immediately reversible, but the specific implications:

Any new threshold that references the "70.1% KEEP accuracy" figure should be treated as a 2022-condition estimate. Current realistic expectation: ~65%.
The VS≥66 + MC<60 boundary confirmed at 71.5% test needs a tier-stratified check before shipping — if those VS≥66 rows are predominantly Challengers, the 71.5% figure could also be overstated relative to what live use will see.
Future walk-forward runs should stratify or at minimum report accuracy by tier, so threshold decisions are made against tier-aware baselines rather than a pooled number that can shift by ~5pp just from backfill composition changes.
