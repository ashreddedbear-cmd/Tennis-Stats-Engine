#178 - Calibration monotonicity & bucket-accuracy check (Step 2 of Task #172)

What & Why
After the orientation fix (Task #175) and the reliability-floor refit
(Step 1, 2026-08-10), model #712 is now the active calibration model.
Before Step 3, we need to confirm two separate things: (1) the
calibration curve is monotone — it should never assign a lower
confidence to a higher raw signal than to a lower one — and (2) the
calibrated output actually tracks observed reality in the buckets that
were previously broken.

Note: calibrated output being below raw x is NOT itself a problem.
Calibration is supposed to correct raw scores in both directions —
pull underconfident predictions up and pull overconfident ones down.
Do not flag calibrated < raw as a reversal on its own; only monotonicity
violations and bucket-accuracy gaps are real findings here.

Done looks like
- Query the validation segment of evaluation_predictions: for each row
  compute orientedX = max(raw/100, 1-raw/100) and calibratedConfidence
  = applyCalibrationOriented(model #712 knots, raw/100)
- Sort by orientedX and confirm calibratedConfidence is monotone
  non-decreasing across the full validation set — flag any x1 < x2
  pair where calibrated(x1) > calibrated(x2)
- For the x=0.66–0.75 bucket (Kostyuk/Swiatek region), compare
  calibrated output against the empirical win rate observed in that
  bucket's training data (not against raw x) — flag if the gap
  exceeds 2pp
- Repeat the bucket-accuracy check for at least 2 other buckets
  spanning the range (e.g. x=0.40–0.50 and x=0.85–0.95) to confirm
  the fix generalizes, not just at the one previously-checked bucket
- Document findings in docs/audit-task172-step2-reversal-check.md
- Update .local/tasks/specialist-calibration-audit-and-fix.md to mark
  Step 2 DONE, or flag specific monotonicity/accuracy violations found
