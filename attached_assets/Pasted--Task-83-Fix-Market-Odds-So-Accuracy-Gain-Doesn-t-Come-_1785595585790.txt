**What & Why**

Section B of the market odds ablation (n=180) found a tension: +1.6pp accuracy with market odds included, but +0.0243 worse log-loss. The original read was that the module over-adjusts raw probabilities. That may still be true — but it may also be that the log-loss number itself was measured through a calibration curve that was never fit on market-odds-inclusive predictions (see calibration-refit task). Tuning the module's weight against that stale number risks landing on a weight that compensates for a measurement bug rather than a real modeling problem — and could throw away real accuracy gain for no reason if the module was never actually miscalibrated.

If the gate above confirms this is still a real issue after refitting, then: the module is shifting picks in the right direction but applying too much force on the underlying probability, producing overconfident outputs — a real risk for a parlay app where leg probabilities compound.
