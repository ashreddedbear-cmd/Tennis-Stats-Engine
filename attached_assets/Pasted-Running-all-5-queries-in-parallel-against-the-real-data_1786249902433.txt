Why are win rates so high at low x, given -1a ruled out a scale bug?

The scale is correct (0–1). The orientation is the issue. In these rows, outcome = (actualWinnerId === player1Id) ? 1 : 0. In the Sackmann-sourced historical data, the match winner is stored as player1. So "model gives player1 only 25% probability" means the model opposed the stored winner — yet the stored winner (player1) still won 97% of the time, because they literally are the winner in the training format. This is real, well-populated data with a systematic labeling convention mismatch versus inference time.

Implication for the "minimum-support floor" hypothesis (leading hypothesis for 0d): The floor would not remove the orientation bias — 3,753 rows back the General flat zone and 3,802 rows back the ATP-Hard flat zone. Keep it as a testable hypothesis, not a settled fix.
